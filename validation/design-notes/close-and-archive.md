# Close and archive with a reason (F-R01-09, Severity 2)

**The department's answer.** Work that dies is cancelled or closed with a
reason, removed from active lists but kept in an archive, recording who, why
and when (R01, S2 task 6 and Q6).

**Conflict.** The prototype has «مرفوض» for a release decision, but no end
state for the work itself: rejected work stays in every list. The status
vocabulary in `MVP_draft.md` section 49 has no closed or archived status, and
the project rules forbid inventing Arabic status terms without a department
verdict. `disscussios/First_idea_darft.md` already ends the department flow
with "Archive".

**Options.**
1. A new status «مغلق» in the status vocabulary.
2. Archive as a property of the work, separate from status: the last status
   stays (for example «مرفوض»), the work leaves active lists, and the closure
   records who, when and the reason.

**Recommendation.** Option 2, in Checkpoint 2.1 (Work and Campaign Engine),
because it keeps the established status terms intact and an archive is a
lifecycle fact, not a review outcome. The Arabic label for the action needs a
verdict: ask the Director at the re-check whether «إغلاق العمل» or «إلغاء
العمل» is the department's word.
