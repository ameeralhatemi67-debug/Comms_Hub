# Stages per content type (F-R01-10, Severity 2)

**The department's answer.** The four fixed production stages do not cover
the design cycle; the department wants to customise them (R01, S4 task 5).

**Conflict.** Every work in the prototype has the same four steps (كتابة النص،
التصوير / التصميم، الإعداد النهائي، التحقق قبل المراجعة). The workflow map
already recorded this as open item O-009 and bottleneck B7.

**Options.**
1. A stage template per content type (post, design, video, press release),
   chosen when the work is created and editable by leadership.
2. Free stages per work, added by whoever is assigned.

**Recommendation.** Option 1 in Checkpoint 2.1, with templates kept by
leadership so stages stay comparable across the team view and the load figures
of task 0.2.3.6. Option 2 makes stage reporting meaningless. Ask the Director
for the real design and video stages at the re-check, so the first templates
come from the department rather than from the agent.
