# Stage deadlines (F-R01-18, Severity 2)

**The department's answer.** Every work has a final deadline; multi-step work
may also have a deadline per stage; simple tasks keep one date (R01, S2
structural question 4). The biggest blockers named are late content from
other departments and late approvals, and the department wants alerts on
delay.

**Conflict.** The prototype has one delivery date per work. Task 0.2.3.5 now
measures overdue against it, but cannot say which stage is late.

**Options.**
1. Optional deadlines on the stages of the stage template (see the note on
   configurable stages); overdue and alerts then work per stage.
2. Separate milestones unrelated to stages.

**Recommendation.** Option 1 in Checkpoint 2.1, built on the same stage
templates, with delay alerts in Checkpoint 2.4. The overdue rule of task
0.2.3.5 extends naturally: a stage is late when it is not done by its date.
