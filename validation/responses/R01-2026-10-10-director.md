### 1.1 Anomaly to resolve before weighting S3 to S7

The form branches on the role question: each role is sent to its own section,
then to the common exit section (`google-form-generator.gs` lines 82 to 99). This
response nevertheless carries answers in all seven role sections. In addition:

- The S1 and S2 notes are long, in the participant's own Gulf dialect, and full of
  operational detail.
- The S3 to S7 notes are short, in formal Arabic, and close to the task wording
  (for example "كتبت صياغة مخصصة لمنصة X منفصلة عن النص الأساسي").
- All structural questions of S3 to S6 are empty.

Two readings fit: the Director walked every role through View As and answered
briefly, or another person completed those sections on the same submission.
**Ask the participant which, before S3 to S7 answers are weighted.** Until then
they are recorded below as findings, but they do not count toward role coverage
under exit gate criterion 2, which allows a participant to count for one role
group only.

## 3. Dimension scores

| D1 fidelity | D2 authority | D3 findability | D4 terminology | D5 trust |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3 |

Evidence for the scores of 3, condensed from the participant: workflow,
findability and terminology get 3 because overdue work cannot be counted, some
file uploads and timestamped notes are unclear, and the work path does not show
the team's load and stages well enough. Trust gets 3 because approval state and
changes need to be shown more clearly.

What surprised the participant: editing content that was already approved sends
the work back to draft, and the six visible items were all past their dates with
no counter for overdue work.

Closing recommendation: show overdue work and each employee's load, clarify the
stages and responsibilities of work, add file upload and timestamped video
notes, and link channel variants to a clear state when the master text changes.

## 5. Answers to the open questions

- **Q1 approval authority:** the Director approves. In future the approval path
  may differ by the sensitivity and type of content. A design-only change needs a
  new approval of the design version, not of unchanged text (see F-R01-04).
- **Q2 life after publication:** the error is assessed and the action decided,
  either editing the post or deleting and republishing it. The Director decides.
  Answers E1.
- **Q3 delegation:** temporary delegation to a named person for the leave period,
  expiring automatically. Answers E2.
- **Q4 urgency:** urgent publication within the hour can happen. The path is
  accelerated and prioritised, final approval stays with the Director before
  publishing, and the request should be markable as urgent. Answers E3.
- **Q5 channel account reality:** not answered (S6 structural questions blank).
- **Q6 work that dies:** close or cancel with a reason, archived and out of active
  lists, recording who, why and when. Answers E11.
- **Q7 intake and campaign shape:** work comes from the charity's operational
  plan, requests from other departments, senior management directives, occasions
  and events, media coverage and urgent requests. A large share is outside
  planned campaigns, especially daily requests and coverage; no exact share is
  known, and the department wants the system to measure planned against
  unplanned work. One piece of content sometimes serves several campaigns.
  Answers E7 and E8.
- **Biggest current blocker (S2-Q5):** late arrival of information and content
  from other departments, repeated edits, late approvals, and urgent requests
  that reshuffle the queue. Wanted: organised intake of requests, a named owner
  per stage, delivery and approval deadlines, and alerts on delay.
